Shipped unmodified, as the two files MiSans-Regular.ttf and MiSans-Bold.ttf.
zz_zh_locale.rpy registers them in renpy.config.font_name_map as the fallback
for every code point range the game's own fonts cannot draw, and in
renpy.config.font_replacement_map so `{b}` runs use the real Bold weight
instead of a synthetically emboldened Regular.
